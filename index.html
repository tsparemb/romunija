1
Ljubljana → Timișoara
Saturday 11 July
Transit
🚗 ~950 km · ~10h · All motorway · Leave 6am

Ljubljana → Zagreb → Budapest → Timișoara. Arrive ~5pm. Fill up in Hungary. Buy Romanian roviniete e-toll before crossing border (roviniete.ro, ~€7/7 days). Buy Hungarian vignette if not held (~€15 annual).

2
Timișoara → Cisnădie
Sunday 12 July · Check-out Timișoara by 11:00 · Check-in Cisnădie from 15:00
Base Move
🚗 ~270 km · ~5h with stops

Timișoara morning (2h max): Piața Victoriei (1989 revolution, bullet holes on façades), Piața Unirii (baroque square, two cathedrals). Short — you drive today.

Corvin Castle, Hunedoara (130km): Most dramatic Gothic-Renaissance castle in Romania. Real drawbridge, towers on a rock. Arrive ~11am, allow 1.5h.

Alba Iulia (50km east): Vauban star fortress, coronation cathedral. 45–60 min. Skip if Corvin ran long.

⚠ Day after 950km. Alba Iulia is skippable without guilt.
3
Sibiu — ASTRA + Old Town
Monday 13 July · Base: Cisnădie (Day 1 of 5) · ~20km to Sibiu centre
Base Day

ASTRA National Museum Complex: Europe's largest open-air museum, 96ha, 300+ original rural structures. Half-day minimum.

Sibiu old town: "Eyes of Sibiu" dormer windows, Large + Small Squares, Bridge of Lies, Lutheran Cathedral tower.

4
Sighișoara + Biertan
Tuesday 14 July · Base: Cisnădie (Day 2 of 5)
Base Day
🚗 ~170 km round trip

Sighișoara (65km): Only continuously inhabited medieval citadel in Europe (UNESCO). Arrive before 9am. Allow 2h.

Biertan (30km back): Most impressive fortified Saxon church in Romania (UNESCO). 1h.

5
Hike — Cindrel Summit
Wednesday 15 July · Base: Cisnădie (Day 3 of 5)
Hike Day
🚗 ~30 km · Păltiniș resort
🥾 Cindrel — Ștefanu Ridge Circuit (2,244m)
From Păltiniș (1,450m), ascend to Cindrel summit then continue along the ridge to Vârful Ștefanu (2,242m) and loop back down a different valley. Proper ridge walking, wide open above the treeline, varied terrain. Mountain restaurant at Păltiniș for lunch on return.
📍 16 km↑ +900m⏱ 5.5–6h● Moderate

Back in Sibiu by late afternoon.

6
Transfăgărășan Road
Thursday 16 July · Base: Cisnădie (Day 4 of 5)
Base Day
🚗 ~190 km round trip

Drive south over the pass (2,042m). 90 hairpin bends, waterfalls, tunnels. Bâlea Lake at the top. Poenari Fortress on south side — real Dracula's castle, 1,480 steps. Return north.

⚠ Check first: transfagarasan.info — road opens ~8am, can close in July.
7
Cisnădie → Transalpina → Brașov
Friday 17 July · Check-out Cisnădie by 10:00 · Check-in Brașov 24h flexible
Base Move
🚗 ~220 km · ~4.5h

Transalpina (DN67C, 2,145m): Romania's highest road. Drive high section then cut east to Brașov. Skip if weather poor — bonus not a must.

8
Brașov — Rest + Explore
Saturday 18 July · Base: Brașov (Day 1 of 6)
Base Day

Rest day after 3 base moves. Black Church (largest Gothic church in SE Europe), Piața Sfatului, Tampa Hill cable car for panoramic views. Evening on the main square.

9
Hike — Postăvaru Summit
Sunday 19 July · Base: Brașov (Day 2 of 6)
Hike Day
🚗 ~25 km · Poiana Brașov
🥾 Postăvaru — Cristianu Mare Circuit (1,799m)
From Poiana Brașov, ascend to Postăvaru summit then continue along the ridge to Cristianu Mare and loop back down. 360° views throughout: Brașov below, Bucegi massif, Piatra Craiului ridge, Făgăraș mountains.
📍 14 km↑ +700m⏱ 5h● Moderate
10
Bear Sanctuary + Bran Castle
Monday 20 July · Base: Brașov (Day 3 of 6)
Base Day
🚗 ~30 km

Libearty Bear Sanctuary, Zărnești (14km): 75+ rescued bears in 70ha forest. Small guided groups. Book max 10 days ahead millions.ro/bears

Bran Castle: Dramatic cliff-top setting. The Dracula connection is mostly marketing but the setting is genuinely impressive and kids enjoy it.

Râșnov Fortress (15km from Bran): Hilltop medieval fortress, kid-friendly, great views over the valley. All three sights are within 15km of each other — natural cluster.

11
Hike — Zărnești Gorge
Tuesday 21 July · Base: Brașov (Day 4 of 6)
Hike Day
🚗 ~50 km · Piatra Craiului NP
🥾 Zărnești Gorge + Piatra Craiului Ridge Approach
Walk the full gorge then continue up toward the Piatra Craiului ridge for proper mountain views above the canyon. The gorge narrows to just a few metres at its tightest point before opening into the dramatic ridge landscape above.
📍 16 km↑ +600m⏱ 5.5h● Moderate
12
Peleș Castle
Wednesday 22 July · Base: Brașov (Day 5 of 6)
Base Day
🚗 ~90 km round trip · Sinaia

Peleș Castle, Sinaia (45km): Most ornate royal palace in Romania, 160 rooms of neo-Renaissance excess. Open Wed from 10am, last entry 4pm. Max 2,000 visitors/day. With Râșnov moved to Day 10, you now have time to do Peleș properly — the interior tour takes 1.5–2h and deserves it. Book ahead peles.ro

Sinaia itself is a pleasant mountain resort town — good lunch options and a monastery worth a quick look.

13
Brașov → Viscri → Gura Humorului
Thursday 23 July · Check-out Brașov 24h · Check-in Gura Humorului 15:00–22:00
Base Move
🚗 ~370 km · ~6h · Leave early

Viscri (60km NE): Most celebrated Saxon village in Transylvania. Arrive before 9am. Allow 1–1.5h.

Prejmer (25km back): 272-cell honeycomb interior (UNESCO). 45 min. Skip if Viscri ran long.

Drive north to Gura Humorului (~260km). Arrive afternoon.

14
Painted Monasteries — Group 1
Friday 24 July · Base: Gura Humorului (Day 1 of 4)
Base Day
🚗 ~80 km

Voroneț (5km): "Voroneț blue" never replicated in 500 years. Last Judgement on entire west wall. 1h.

Humor (6km): Warm reddish-brown tones. Moldovița (60km): Largest, fortified enclosure, Siege of Constantinople fresco.

15
Painted Monasteries — Group 2
Saturday 25 July · Base: Gura Humorului (Day 2 of 4)
Base Day
🚗 ~90 km

Sucevița (45km): Best preserved, Tree of Jesse fresco. Arbore (30km): Smallest, most intimate, green tones.

Suceava Fortress: Ruined citadel. Ciocănești: Elaborately painted house façades.

16
Hike — Rarău + Bicaz Gorge
Sunday 26 July · Base: Gura Humorului (Day 3 of 4) · Leave 7:30am
Hike Day
🚗 ~160 km driving
🥾 Rarău — Pietrele Doamnei (1,651m)
Dramatic dolomite towers in Bucovina forested hills. Trailhead at Câmpulung Moldovenesc (30km).
📍 8 km↑ +500m⏱ 3h● Moderate

Bicaz Gorge (60km south): 8km canyon, 300m walls. Red Lake (5km further): Submerged forest, hire a rowboat.

⚠ Long day. Gorge + Red Lake can be cut if energy runs low.
17
Gura Humorului → Putna → Sighet
Monday 27 July · Check-out by 11:00 · Check-in Sighet 16:00–19:00 ⚠ Leave by 12:00
Base Move
🚗 ~160 km · ~3h

Putna Monastery (20km from base): Stefan cel Mare's greatest monastery, founded 1466. Allow 45 min.

Drive northwest over Prislop Pass (1,416m) into Maramureș. Stop at the top for views.

Săpânța-Peri Monastery (15km before Sighet): The tallest wooden church in the world — 75m spire, completed in 2003 in traditional Maramureș style. Extraordinary sight on a hill above the valley. 30 min stop.

Arrive Sighet by 16:00 for check-in window.

18
Wooden Churches Trail
Tuesday 28 July · Base: Sighet (Day 1 of 4)
Base Day
🚗 ~60 km

Full UNESCO wooden church circuit — all 6 key churches in one day:

Bârsana Monastery (20km): Most picturesque, active monastery, extraordinary carved wooden gate. Ieud Hill Church (15km): Oldest wooden church in Romania, 1364, original frescoes. Desești + Budești: Classic Maramureș spire style. Rozavlea: Elegant proportions, 18th century. Șurdești: One of the tallest historic wooden spires in Maramureș.

All built without a single nail, spires up to 50m tall. Allow 20–30 min each. Done by mid-afternoon.

Evening: walk Sighet old town.

19
Breb Village + Hike Dealul Solovan
Wednesday 29 July · Base: Sighet (Day 2 of 4)
Hike Day
🚗 ~30 km to Breb

Drive to Breb village (20km). Horse carts, haystacks, old women in traditional dress. Genuinely living 19th-century rural life.

🥾 Dealul Solovan Ridge
Views over the Mara valley. Ask locals for the path — no formal signage.
📍 8 km↑ +400m⏱ 3h● Easy–Moderate

Return to Sighet for the night.

20
Vaser Valley Steam Railway + Merry Cemetery
Thursday 30 July · Base: Sighet (Day 3 of 4)
Base Day
🚗 ~60 km + 5–6h on the train

Vaser Valley Railway (Vișeu de Sus, 35km): Working 1932 steam locomotive, 22km into wilderness. Thursday ✅ train runs. Be at station 20–30 min early. Book weeks ahead cffviseu.ro

Merry Cemetery, Săpânța (15km from Vișeu): Hand-carved crosses with humorous epitaphs. Unique on Earth. Allow 1.5h.

21
Sighet → Sovata → Cluj/Florești
Friday 31 July · Check-out Sighet by 12:00 · Check-in Cluj from 14:00
Base Move
🚗 ~280 km · ~5h with stop

Sovata / Lacul Ursu (~120km): Heliothermal salt lake — cold surface, warmer deeper, effortless floating. Allow 1.5–2h. Bring old swimwear — salt stains.

Continue to Cluj/Florești (~120km, fast motorway). Arrive early-mid afternoon.

22
Salina Turda + Turda Gorge
Saturday 1 August · Base: Cluj/Florești (Day 1 of 3)
Base Day
🚗 ~60 km

Salina Turda (30km): Underground salt mine amusement park, 120m below surface. Ferris wheel, boat on subterranean lake, amphitheatre. Nothing like it in Europe. Book ahead salinaturda.ro. Allow 2–3h.

🥾 Turda Gorge circuit
Afternoon after the mine. Dramatic limestone gorge 5km from Salina. The full circuit goes up over the ridge and back through the gorge floor — ridge section has some exposure which adds excitement.
📍 8 km loop↑ +350m⏱ 3h● Moderate
23
Hike — Székelykő Ridge above Rimetea
Sunday 2 August · Base: Cluj/Florești (Day 2 of 3)
Hike Day
🚗 ~90 km · ~2h on slow roads
🥾 Székelykő Ridge above Rimetea
Limestone escarpment above a beautifully preserved white Hungarian village. 360° views over the Apuseni landscape.
📍 7–8 km↑ +450m⏱ 3h● Moderate

Lunch in Rimetea village before heading back.

Cheile Aiudului (Aiud Gorge, 15km from Rimetea): Dramatic limestone gorge, completely off the tourist trail. Short walk through the canyon — wild and uncrowded. 1h. Perfectly placed between Rimetea and the motorway back to Cluj.

24
Cluj → Oradea → Debrecen
Monday 3 August · Check-out Cluj by 11:00 · Check-in Debrecen evening
Base Move
🚗 ~280 km · ~4h with stop

Oradea (130km west): Romania's great Art Nouveau city. Black Eagle Palace, Moon Church, Canonic Row. Allow 1.5–2h.

Cross border to Debrecen (~100km). Arrive early evening. Any hotel — purely functional.

25
Debrecen → Ljubljana
Tuesday 4 August
Transit
🚗 ~650 km · ~6.5h · All motorway · Leave mid-morning

Debrecen → Budapest → Zagreb → Ljubljana. Home by early afternoon.
